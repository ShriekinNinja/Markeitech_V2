## Canonical Coverage Decision

`markeitech-zero-dte-risk-expert` owns only candidate-level risk synthesis, risk-review validity,
stop conditions, unresolved-risk inventory, and disclosure for supplied long, single-leg 0DTE
candidates. It consumes canonical facts from Options Intelligence and adjacent specialists. It is
not a general options authority and must not become a second owner of candidate quality,
affordability, tradeability, thesis, portfolio risk, proposal ranking, or execution.

## Consultation Order

When material to the question:

1. options-market mechanics establishes exact series, exchange, exercise, settlement, quotation,
   and provider-field semantics;
2. provider and Nautilus specialists establish delivery prerequisites and adapter exposure;
3. data quality and quantitative validation determine their separate dispositions, then evidence
   fitness determines whether quote, Greek, IV, formula, scenario, timestamp,
   and lineage evidence is admissible for the use;
4. zero-DTE risk synthesizes the risk consequences and review validity; and
5. the advisory owner may consume the bounded review without treating it as ranking, suitability,
   or permission to trade.

If a required owner is unavailable, return `REQUIRED_CONSULTATION` or `NOT_VERIFIED`; do not let
the risk advisor fill the missing column.
