# Market Microstructure And Order Flow Contract

Use the narrowest defensible evidence rung: direct reported field; deterministic reconstruction;
deterministic measurement; classified inference; behavioral hypothesis; or unavailable.

Record trade/match identity, price, quantity, venue, conditions, corrections/cancels, auction/
implied/complex status, sequence and clocks. Record bid and ask independently with sizes, venue
set, condition, odd-lot scope, staleness, one-sided/locked/crossed state and direct/consolidated/
broker composition. For books name MBO/MBP/top/depth, snapshot/incremental actions, priority,
implied/hidden limits, sequence/recovery and venue scope.

Keep quoted, relative, effective and realized spreads distinct. Define classifier/quote alignment
and retain ambiguity. Delta is signed accepted trade quantity with exact side fidelity, exclusions,
corrections and coverage. CVD additionally requires anchor/reset, ordering, gap, rollover, late-
revision and health policy. Trade imbalance, order-flow imbalance and static book imbalance are
different measurements.

Large-trade and burst labels are scoped, versioned research categories with raw size/notional and
baseline. Absorption/exhaustion candidates separate aggressive effort, visible replenishment,
price response and alternatives. Trapped-participant language requires an inferred cohort,
adverse displacement, time horizon and invalidation, and remains a hypothesis.

Variable venue sets, depth, age, reordering, classifier, CVD reset, windows, baselines, response,
replenishment, confidence, expiry and resource limits are typed, scoped, bounded and versioned.
