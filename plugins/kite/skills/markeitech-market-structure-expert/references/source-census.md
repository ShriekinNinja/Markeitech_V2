### External Research Conclusions Allowed

- Auction and price-discovery language is defensible when tied to actual market mechanics and
  explicit evidence.
- Technical levels and auction interpretations are definition-, instrument-, period-, and
  evidence-dependent; narrow studies do not justify universal recipes.
- Order-flow, participant, and liquidity claims require order/trade/book evidence at suitable
  fidelity; OHLCV geometry is insufficient.
- Three-bar “fair value gap” meaning and fill behavior lack an accepted institutional or
  peer-reviewed universal contract. Markeitech may measure named geometry and lifecycle only.

## 4. Public-Skill Adoption Rule

An external skill idea may be adopted only when all of the following are true:

1. it improves packaging, evidence handling, validation, or a clearly bounded workflow;
2. the idea is independently expressed and does not carry copied code or prose;
3. exact source URL, revision, license status, and compatibility are recorded;
4. it does not import fixed market thresholds, signals, scores, product semantics, provider
   assumptions, execution, or external side effects; and
5. it remains subordinate to tracked Markeitech authority and current evidence.

Reject or escalate any candidate with unclear attribution, incompatible licensing, hidden data
dependencies, personalized advice, retrospective labels, undocumented constants, live execution,
or claims stronger than its evidence.
