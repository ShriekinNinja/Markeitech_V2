# Visual Evidence Review

**Status:** Passive review procedure; disabled in the active V3 profile unless explicitly enabled

Visual review is a diagnostic projection of canonical deterministic evidence. It helps a human
inspect geometry, timestamps, identity, warmup, health, fidelity, and revision behavior. It is not
market truth, runtime readiness, an acceptance shortcut, a dashboard commitment, or a Sir Loke
interface.

## Non-Interference Boundary

The visual path may consume only immutable admitted projections or deterministic fixtures. It may
render and capture them, but it must not:

- subscribe to a provider or request historical data directly;
- create or revise bars, measurements, entities, events, opportunities, broker facts, or policy;
- write product state or raw observations to PostgreSQL;
- supply a required readiness prerequisite;
- run blocking browser/render work on the Nautilus event loop;
- hide missing, stale, degraded, unsupported, inferred, or conflicting evidence; or
- turn visual resemblance or a profitable example into semantic validation.

A visualization failure stays local. Core market acquisition, analysis, broker observation,
persistence, Discord, and future Sir Loke operation must continue according to their own health.

## Required Review Identity

Every review item records enough identity to reproduce and interpret it:

- capability/contract, schema, producer, formula/definition, parameter, configuration, and policy
  versions;
- instrument, venue, exact contract, evidence source, selector, resolution, session/calendar,
  trade date, horizon, and relevant UTC bounds;
- fixture or connected-capture source, immutable content hash, generation command, checkout commit,
  and tool/runtime versions;
- event, receive, calculation, publication, and capture timestamps where they affect meaning;
- health, fidelity, coverage, warmup, reasons, corrections/conflicts, and known missing inputs; and
- reviewer, review time, disposition, notes, and linked defect or acceptance evidence.

Do not compare images whose identities or evidence envelopes differ without naming the difference.

## Review Procedure

1. Confirm the capability is implemented, enabled in the reviewed profile, and represented by a
   current deterministic fixture or separately authorized connected capture.
2. Validate the structured source data before rendering: schema, identity, units, timestamps,
   health/fidelity, bounds, revisions, and expected invariants.
3. Generate the view through the approved isolated visual path; record exact inputs and versions.
4. Inspect legibility, scale, time/session alignment, occlusion, missing-data display, status
   labeling, and correspondence between rendered marks and structured values.
5. Exercise at least normal, warming/partial, stale/degraded, unsupported/missing, duplicate, and
   conflict/correction cases which apply to the capability.
6. Record one of `ACCEPTED_FOR_BOUNDED_REVIEW`, `DEFECT`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, with
   the exact scope and evidence.

Disabled, deferred, or pure-contract capabilities are documented as such. Do not generate a fake
runtime screenshot merely to fill an inventory row.

## Acceptance Meaning

A visual acceptance proves only that the reviewed renderer faithfully displayed the identified
inputs inside the recorded envelope and remained operationally isolated. It does not prove:

- provider completeness, consolidated feeds, market-session behavior, or live freshness;
- analytical correctness beyond the separately validated structured contract;
- broker, Discord, persistence, performance, or failure-recovery behavior;
- usefulness across instruments, sessions, regimes, or product decisions; or
- that a disabled/deferred capability is ready for active composition.
